# Limitations and Ethical Considerations

## 1. What NDVI Does NOT Tell Us
*   **NDVI is not a disease detector.** A low NDVI value could mean water stress, nutrient deficiency, pest damage, or simply that the field has been harvested. It cannot distinguish between these causes without field verification.
*   **Spatial Resolution:** Sentinel-2 has a 10-meter resolution. A single pixel (10x10 meters) may contain a mixture of crops, bare soil, and shadows. It is not a precise measurement of a single smallholder farm.

## 2. Atmospheric and Environmental Limitations
*   **Cloud Cover:** Western Kenya experiences heavy cloud cover, especially during the long rains (March–May). This means valid observations may be infrequent during critical growing periods.
*   **Seasonality:** Crop phenology (growth stages) heavily impacts NDVI. Comparing a healthy mature crop to a freshly planted field will yield misleading results without crop calendars.

## 3. Ethical Use
*   This platform is designed as a **decision-support tool**, not an automated diagnostic tool. It highlights areas that require field inspection; it does not replace an agronomist.
*   Field boundaries and farm data uploaded by users should be treated as private information and never shared without explicit consent.
